Identity

Cadence is the rhythm a team finds when work flows: plan, build, ship, repeat. The mark is three beats rising, and the last one lands in Ember, the moment work ships.

Construction

16-unit grid, 64 × 64 viewBox, corner radius 16. Bars are 8 wide with 5-unit gaps and rise 16 → 26 → 34 from a shared baseline. The bars stay optically centred, not mathematically.

Sizes & app icon

Minimum size 16 px (favicon). Below 24 px, the lockup drops the wordmark. Clear space around the mark equals one bar height (16 units).

Usage

Use the full-color mark on light or dark neutrals.

Don't recolor the tile or the beat. Tempo and Ember only.

Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the mark.

Voice

Calm

Work tools are loud enough. We state what happened and what to do next, without exclamation marks or alarm.

"Moved to In review. Undo"
"Awesome!! Your issue was moved!!"

Precise

Name the object and the number. Engineers read our UI, so we never round away the facts.

"3 issues are blocked by ATL-142"
"Some items may need attention"

Human

Plain words, sentence case, second person. Errors explain how to recover, never whose fault it was.

"That link has expired. Ask Ada for a new one."
"Error 410: Token Invalid"
