Card anatomy

Priority, key and assignee on the first line. Then the title, which gets as much width as it needs. Then meta that only appears when it matters: labels, points, sub-issue progress, a linked pull request, a due date turning amber then red.

Live

Presence dots show who has the board open. A teammate's drag shows their cursor and a ghost card. Anything that changes under you gets a one-second Tempo outline, never a full reload. Built on SignalR (M5).

Limits

Column headers show count and WIP limit (3 / 4). A column at its limit tints its header amber. Dropping past the limit is allowed, but it asks for a reason, which appears in the sprint report.
