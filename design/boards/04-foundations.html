Foundations

A 4 px rhythm, soft-but-not-bubbly corners, and borders before shadows. Motion is short and decelerating: it confirms what happened and never makes anyone wait.

Things arrive fast and settle softly. Live updates from teammates fade in with a brief Tempo highlight. prefers-reduced-motion turns every transition into an instant change.

Iconography

Lucide: open-source (ISC), tree-shaken per icon. 16 px in the UI, 1.75 stroke, rounded caps. Icons label nothing on their own: they sit next to text, or carry an aria-label and a tooltip. Status and priority glyphs are custom, drawn on the same 14 px grid.
