Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and titles.
  • The UI base is 14 px. Long-form text (descriptions, comments) is 16 px.
  • Headings tighten tracking as they grow; body text keeps the default.
  • Long-form text stops at 72 characters per line.

Numbers

  • Tabular figures in tables, counters, charts and timers, so columns don't jitter.
  • Relative time under a week ("3 d ago"), then absolute dates ("Oct 14").
  • Units are lighter and smaller than the number they follow.

Performance

  • Two variable WOFF2 files, Latin subset: 52 KB in total (29 + 23), cached immutably.
  • font-display: swap, with metric-matched fallbacks so the layout doesn't shift when fonts arrive.
  • Geist is preloaded. Mono loads on first use.
